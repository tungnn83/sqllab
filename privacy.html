Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how SQLLab ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the SQLLab VS Code extension ("the Extension"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information associated with your use of our software.

1. Information We Collect

SQLLab is designed to operate primarily on your local machine. We collect minimal information:

  • License key: When you activate a paid plan, your license key is stored locally on your machine and verified locally. No internet connection is required after activation.
  • Purchase information: If you purchase a subscription through our payment provider (Lemon Squeezy), your payment and billing details are processed and stored by Lemon Squeezy, not by us. Please refer to their privacy policy for details.
  • Contact information: If you contact us via the VS Code Marketplace Q&A tab or email, we may retain your message and contact details to respond to your inquiry.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

SQLLab does not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your SQL queries or scripts
  • Your database credentials, connection strings, or connection details
  • Query results or any data retrieved from your databases
  • Your saved queries, version history, or any content you create inside the Extension
  • Usage analytics, telemetry, or crash reports

All data you work with inside SQLLab stays entirely on your local machine.

3. Local Data Storage

SQLLab stores the following data locally on your machine only:

  • Database connection profiles (encrypted where supported by your OS)
  • Saved queries, version history, and tags
  • Extension settings and preferences
  • License activation status

This data is never transmitted to our servers.

4. Third-Party Payment Processing

Subscription payments are processed by Lemon Squeezy. When you purchase a plan, you interact directly with Lemon Squeezy's checkout flow. We receive confirmation that a valid license has been issued, but we do not receive or store your payment card details. Please review Lemon Squeezy's Privacy Policy for details on how they handle your payment information.

5. Cookies

This website (the landing page you are reading) does not use cookies or tracking scripts. The SQLLab VS Code extension does not use cookies.

6. Children's Privacy

SQLLab is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised policy on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Extension after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the Q&A tab on the VS Code Marketplace page.
